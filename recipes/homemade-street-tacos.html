Homemade Street Tacos

Have you ever had those tiny tacos from a taco stand and wanted to know how you could bring those home to your kitchen if so keep on reading.

Ingredients

  • Meat-You can use your choice of meat here but it's recommended to use properly seasoned meat (there are plenty of carne asada, chicken tinga, or other recipes out there to accomplish this).
  • Avocado-Adds creaminess to the tacos.
  • Tortillas-Your going to want r 4-5 inch "street taco" tortillas.
  • Cilantro-Adds a pop of freshness
  • Jalapeños-Makes these tacos spicy. This can be left out altogether if need be.
  • Onion-adds a bit of crunch and flavor(you could use sweet or whatever your preference is).
  • Cheese-Cotija cheese is good for these tacos, but feta, queso fresco, or goat cheese provide a similar texture. For a more classic taste, you could opt for cheddar or mexican blend.
  1. Grill the carne asada and cook the chicken tinga according to the recipe instructions.
  2. Slice the carne asada and shred the chicken tinga.
  3. Mash the avocado with a fork. Add in a sprinkle of salt and pepper and a squeeze of lime juice and stir. 1-2 avocados will do.
  4. Fill each tortilla with a spread of mashed avocado and a scoop of meat.
  5. Top the tacos with fresh cilantro, jalapeños, onion, and your choice of cheese. Finish with a squeezeof lime juice over the top.
